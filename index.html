3Crop the main video

Drag the box to move it, drag the corner dots to resize. The crop is applied only between the two crop times; before and after, the full video is shown. Leave a time blank to crop from the beginning / until the end.
Time-only cutting is fast (no re-encoding) but snaps to the nearest keyframe. Cropping the picture always re-encodes, using the fastest settings.
